How Much Is Your Old IT Equipment Worth?

September 2026 5 min read By Sustainable Scrap Services

It's one of the first questions every business asks before a clearout: what is this old equipment actually worth? The honest answer is "it depends" — but there are a handful of factors that consistently drive value up or down, and knowing them helps you get a fair quote.

The main factors that drive value

  • Component type — CPUs, RAM and enterprise drives generally hold more value than casings or peripherals.
  • Condition — working units are worth more than dead ones, though even non-working hardware retains material value.
  • Age and generation — newer server-grade and enterprise components command higher prices than older consumer gear.
  • Quantity — bulk lots are often easier to move and can attract better per-unit rates than single items.
  • Current market rates — metal and component prices fluctuate, so valuations reflect the market on the day.

Rough value tiers by equipment type

EquipmentTypical value driver
Enterprise serversCPUs, RAM capacity, and drive count usually matter more than the chassis itself.
Laptops & desktopsWorking condition and RAM/storage specs; screens and batteries affect resale separately.
Hard disks & SSDsStorage capacity and enterprise vs consumer grade, once data has been securely wiped.
RAM modulesCapacity and generation (DDR3 vs DDR4 vs DDR5) drive per-stick pricing.
CPUsServer-grade Xeon and similar processors typically outvalue consumer chips by a wide margin.

Why bulk lots often value better

A buyer who can collect a full office's worth of equipment in one visit saves on logistics compared to ten separate small pickups, and that efficiency is usually reflected in the rate offered. If you're planning multiple small disposals, it's often worth timing them together.

Getting an accurate valuation

The fastest way to get a realistic number is to send photos and rough quantities directly — model numbers where visible, approximate unit count, and condition (working, partially working, or dead). A same-day estimate is usually possible from that alone, with a final figure confirmed on-site at pickup.

Frequently asked questions

Does the price change between quote and pickup?

Final pricing is confirmed on-site once items are inspected, but our estimates are based on real market rates so changes are usually minor.

Are broken laptops worth anything?

Yes — even non-working units typically have value in their RAM, storage, and internal components.

Do you pay more for bulk lots?

Larger, consolidated lots are often more efficient to collect and grade, which can be reflected in the offer.
